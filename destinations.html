Day trips from Astana

Two natural escapes that fit into a single day, both reachable with our transport and a packed lunch from the city.

Nature · 250 km

Burabay National Park

Pine forests, granite rocks and clear lakes also known as the "Kazakh Switzerland". We leave the city at 08:00 and return after sunset.

  • Boat trip on lake Borovoe
  • Hike to the Okzhetpes rock viewpoint
  • Lunch in a lakeside ethno village

Wildlife · 130 km

Korgalzhyn Nature Reserve

A UNESCO steppe reserve and the northernmost nesting place of pink flamingos. Binoculars and a ranger guide are included in the trip.

  • Bird watching with a local ranger
  • Visit to the steppe museum in Korgalzhyn village
  • Photo stop on the open steppe road

Good to know

Practical tips before you go

Getting around

Buses cost 140 ₸, Yandex and inDrive taxis are cheap and easy to order.

Food

Try baursaks with tea for breakfast and beshbarmak for a big family dinner.

What to pack

A warm jacket even in summer: steppe evenings are famously cool and windy.

Etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees in mosques; shoes come off at the entrance.
