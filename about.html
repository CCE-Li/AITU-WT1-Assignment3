About Astana Explorer

We are a small student team from Astana IT University and we show guests the capital of Kazakhstan.

From a university assignment to a travel guide

Our team moved to Astana from different regions of Kazakhstan to study, and we spent our first semesters exploring the city between lectures. In 2025 we collected our notes, photos and favourite routes into one project.

Today Astana Explorer helps exchange students and tourists to see the best parts of the city:

  1. 2025, semester 1: the team meets and starts a photo diary of the city.
  2. 2025, summer: first three walking routes tested with exchange students.
  3. 2026, spring: day trips to Burabay and Korgalzhyn added to the website.
  4. 2026, autumn: the website becomes our Web Technologies midterm project.

Three rules we never break

Honest advice

If a place is closed or a tour is not worth the money, we say it directly.

Respect for locals

We ask guests to follow mosque dress codes and keep the steppe clean.

Learning by doing

Every new page of this website is first a lesson and then a product.

Astana in numbers

Useful facts before you pack your suitcase:

  • Founded: 1830, as Akmolinsk
  • Capital since: 1997
  • Population: about 1.4 million people
  • Area: 722 square kilometres on the Ishim river
  • Climate: sharp continental, from -25 to +35 degrees
