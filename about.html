About Astana Explorer

We are a small student team from Astana IT University who believe the youngest capital of Central Asia deserves a travel guide written with real love for the city.

Our story

From a university assignment to a real travel guide

Astana changed its name, its skyline and its rhythm several times in the last thirty years. Our team moved here from different regions of Kazakhstan to study, and we spent our first semesters exploring the city between lectures.

In 2025 we collected our notes, photos and favourite routes into one project. Today Astana Explorer helps exchange students, tourists and locals see the capital without missing its best parts.

  1. 2025 · Semester 1 The team meets at Astana IT University and starts a photo diary of the city.
  2. 2025 · Summer First three walking routes tested with exchange students from our dormitory.
  3. 2026 · Spring Day trips to Burabay and Korgalzhyn added, together with a booking form.
  4. 2026 · Autumn The website becomes our Web Technologies midterm project and goes online.
Our values

Three rules we never break

Honest advice

If a place is closed for renovation or a tour is not worth the money, we say so directly in the description.

Respect for locals

We ask guests to follow mosque dress codes, support small cafes and keep the steppe clean on every route.

Learning by doing

The project is part of our studies: every new page, layout or feature is first a lesson, then a product.

The city at a glance

Astana in numbers

Useful facts before you pack your suitcase — collected from official city sources and our own experience.

Key facts about Astana
Founded 1830 (as Akmolinsk)
Capital since 1997
Population about 1.4 million people
Area 722 km² on the Ishim river
Climate sharp continental, −25°C to +35°C
Districts Almaty, Bayterek, Esil, Nura, Saryarka
120+ modern landmarks built since 1997
14 parks and public gardens in the city
2 200 h of sunshine per year on average
3 h by car to Burabay National Park
