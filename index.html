Plan your visit

A capital that is easy to love in any season

Astana sits in the middle of the steppe, which means bright summers, snowy winters and very clear blue skies all year round. Pick a season and we will adapt the route for you.

  • Summer (June – August): river cruises, parks and evening walks along the embankment.
  • Winter (December – February): ice towns, museums and the famous snowy skyline.
  • Shoulder months: fewer tourists, lower prices and the best light for photos.
