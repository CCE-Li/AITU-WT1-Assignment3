Plan your visit in any season

Astana is in the middle of the steppe, so summers are bright and winters are snowy. Pick a season and we will adapt the route for you.

  • Summer: river cruises, parks and evening walks along the embankment.
  • Winter: ice towns, museums and the famous snowy skyline.
  • Spring and autumn: fewer tourists and the best light for photos.
