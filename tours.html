Frequently asked questions

How do I pay for a tour?

We confirm your booking by email first, then you can pay in cash on the day of the tour.

What happens if it rains or snows?

City tours run in any weather. Day trips can be moved to another date free of charge.

Can you pick us up from the airport?

Yes, the transfer is free for the Culture Pass package and costs 3000 tenge for other tours.

Are the tours suitable for children?

Yes. City Lights and the museums are perfect for families, and children from 6 years old can join the day trips.
